《大过》<br>
大楇-收丝 (Spindle) - 大輠-承膏 (Lubrication)<br>
<br>
栋桡，利有攸往，亨。<br>
天栋星被搅扰[^1]。利于有所前往，亨通。<br>
The Arcturus is disturbed. It is favorable to go somewhere; there is success.<br>
参考义项：猾，播弄，扰乱（通滑，用同榾）<br>
<br>
《象》曰：泽灭木，《大过》。君子以独立不惧，遁世无闷。<br>
过_交往-特_孤立：泽水淹没树木，这就是大过。君子由此审慎确立而不畏惧，长久继承而不烦闷。<br>
Contact-Isolate: The marsh submerges the tree; this is Da Guo. Thus the noble one establishes with prudence yet without fear, and maintains the succession over the long term without becoming weary.<br>
<br>
初六：藉用白茅，无咎。<br>
给予：用洁白的茅草铺垫承托，没有过错。<br>
Giving: Use white rushes as a mat and support. There is no blame.<br>
<br>
九二：枯杨生稊，老夫得其女妻，无不利。<br>
过活：枯老的杨树生出嫩芽，年老的丈夫感恩其柔弱的妻子，无所不利。<br>
Living: A withered poplar puts forth tender shoots; an aging husband feels gratitude toward his frail wife. Nothing is unfavorable.<br>
<br>
九三：栋桡，凶。<br>
祸害：屋梁挠曲，凶险。<br>
Harm: The ridgepole is bent under pressure. Misfortune.<br>
<br>
九四：栋隆，吉。有它，吝。<br>
转移：屋梁向上隆起，吉祥；长久驮负，憾惜。<br>
Transfer: The ridgepole arches upward. Auspicious. Bearing the load for too long brings regret.<br>
<br>
九五：枯杨生华，老妇得其士夫，无咎无誉。<br>
过分：枯老的杨树开花，年老的妇人立依赖她的成年儿子为继嗣；没有过错。没有党与。<br>
Excess: A withered poplar blooms; an elderly woman designates her adult son who remains dependent on his mother as heir. There is no blame. There is no alliance.<br>
<br>
上六：过涉灭顶，凶。无咎。<br>
失去：涉水过深，水没头顶；凶险，但没有过错。<br>
Loss: One wades too far and the water rises over the head. Misfortune, but no blame.<br>
<br>
[^1]: 天栋星以栋梁为名，代表天王的帝廷，与治理有关。<br>
The Arcturus known in Chinese as the star of the Heavenly Beam—named for the concept of a structural beam—represents the imperial court of the Celestial Sovereign and is associated with governance.<br>
