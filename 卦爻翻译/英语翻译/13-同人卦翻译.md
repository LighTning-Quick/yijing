《同人》<br>
尊重人 (Respecting People) - 通仁：施行仁德 (Practice Benevolence)<br>
<br>
同人于野，亨。利涉大川。利君子贞。<br>
在荒野与他人沟通结交，通达。利于涉过大河，利于君子守持正固。<br>
Communicating and forging connections with others in the wilderness, brings success. It is favorable to cross the great river, and favorable for the noble one to hold fast to what is upright.<br>
参考义项：勇，悍勇，勇敢。<br>
<br>
《象》曰：天与火，同人。君子以类族辨物。<br>
戾-率领：天与火相与，这就是同人。君子以此遵循族属治理万物。<br>
Herd: Heaven together with fire forms Tongren. Therefore the noble one governs things according to their clan affiliation.<br>
<br>
初九：同人于门，无咎。<br>
威重：在大门通告人，没有过错。<br>
Gravity / Accord: One announces the people at the gate; there is no blame.<br>
<br>
六二：同人于宗，吝。<br>
通-交往：在宗族施行仁德（亲仁），会有局促与憾惜。<br>
Engage / Concord: One practices benevolence within the clan (kin-centered humaneness), there will be constraint and regret.<br>
<br>
九三：伏戎于莽，升其高陵，三岁不兴。<br>
疏通：在草莽中驾驭兵车，要登上高陵，长久不能兴兵。<br>
Dredge / Discordance: One drives war chariots through the overgrowth and seeks to ascend the high ridge, yet for a long time cannot raise the troops.<br>
<br>
九四：乘其墉，弗克攻，吉。<br>
慎重：登上对方的城墙，却没能攻占，吉。<br>
Prudence / Record: One climbs the other side's wall yet fails to capture it; this is auspicious.<br>
<br>
九五：同人先号咷而后笑，大师克相遇。<br>
统一：会合人，先哭号，后笑；大帅（克敌之后）终得相遇。<br>
Unification / Coordinator: Rendezvous; first cry out and weep, then laugh. Generals can finally meet (after overcoming the enemy).<br>
<br>
上九：同人于郊，无悔。<br>
触动：因郊祀使人劳动，没有悔恨。<br>
Move / Cardiac Transplant: The suburban sacrifice entails labor for the people; there is no regret.<br>
