《习坎》<br>
学习借鉴 (Learning and Drawing Lessons) - 习监：训练监督 (Training and Supervision)<br>
<br>
有孚维心，亨。行有尚。<br>
（阴险）使人信孚，算计内心，通达。（约束、检查）通过阻挡来使之离开。<br>
(Insidious) Winning others' trust, harboring calculated, ulterior motives; success. (Restraining and examining) Steering clear through blocking.<br>
参考义项：憸，奸邪阴险；检，约束，检查<br>
<br>
《象》曰：水洊至，习坎。君子以常德行，习教事。<br>
敛_整肃-详_审查：水流相续而至，这就是习坎。君子通过引导德行，训练效力职事。<br>
Rectify-Review: The water flows continuously, and this is Xikan. The noble one, by guiding moral conduct, trains the fulfillment of duty.<br>
<br>
初六：习坎，入于坎窞，凶。<br>
敛藏：（给死人）穿袭衣入殓，放入墓穴深坑，凶险。<br>
Hide / Trouble: Dressing the deceased in graveclothes for encoffining, putting into the burial pit; misfortune.<br>
<br>
九二：坎有险，求小得。<br>
敛-停止：用艰险磨砺，最终暂时满意。<br>
Halt / Traverse: Tempered by perils and hardships, ultimately achieving a sense of temporary satisfaction.<br>
<br>
六三：来之坎坎，险且枕，入于坎窞，勿用。<br>
泛滥：勤勉挖坑，幽深且深陷，落入坑深处，不可有所作为。<br>
Overflow / Trench: Diligently digging a pit—deep and steep—only to fall to its very bottom; not to accomplish anything.<br>
<br>
六四：樽酒，簋贰用缶，纳约自牖，终无咎。<br>
敛聚：以樽盛酒，用陶缶向簋添饭食，通过窗户放进绳子。终无过错。<br>
Gather / Trough: Fill a vessel with wine, use an earthenware jar to add food to the ritual bowl, and lower items in through the window using a rope. In the end, there will be no fault.<br>
<br>
九五：坎不盈，祗既平，无咎。<br>
鉴-审查：瘗坎不盈满，（填埋后的）土坻仅得持平，无过错。<br>
Scrutinize / Tempering: The sacrificial pit is not filled; the earthen mound after burial is only level. There is no blame.<br>
<br>
上六：系用徽纆，寘于丛棘，三岁不得，凶。<br>
喊-呵责：被绳索捆绑，弃置在荆棘丛中，三年不满足，凶险。<br>
Berate / Tribulation: Bound with cords and placed among thorns, not satisfied for three years; misfortune.<br>
