《观》<br>
观阙 (Gate Tower) - 台榭 (Terrace-Spectacle)<br>
<br>
盥而不荐。有孚颙若。<br>
洗手灌祭而不献上牲牢。肃敬的样子使人信从。<br>
Wash hands for the libation but do not present the sacrificial offerings. Reverent demeanor inspires compliance.<br>
参考义项：灌祭（祼礼）<br>
<br>
《象》曰：风行地上，《观》。先王以省方观民设教。<br>
旌-表明：风行在地上，这就是观卦。先王通过省视四方，来示范于民、施用教化。<br>
Indicate: The wind moves over the earth; this is Guan. The former kings would inspect the regions to set the example for the people and disseminate moral instruction.<br>
<br>
初六：童观，小人无咎，君子吝。<br>
劝-勤勉：劳作操持，小人没有错咎，君子有吝惜。<br>
Diligence: Labor and management; for the petty person there is no blame, but for the noble one there is regret.<br>
《象》曰："初六童观"，"小人"道也。<br>
《象》传说："初六劳作操持"，是小人的道。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "The bottom six, labor and management" is the way of the petty person.<br>
<br>
六二：窥观，利女贞。<br>
观察：窥看，利于女子贞正。<br>
Observation: Peeping observation; favorable for a woman's steadfast.<br>
《象》曰："窥观女贞"，亦可丑也。<br>
《象》传说："窥观女贞"，也是可比同的。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "Peeping observation; favorable for a woman's steadfast" can also become the same.<br>
<br>
六三：观我，生进退。<br>
借鉴：观察我，反省进退。<br>
Refer: Observe myself, reflecting on advance and retreat (my actions).<br>
《象》曰："观我生进退"，未失道也。<br>
《象》传说："观察我，反省进退"，没有失去正道。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "Observe myself, reflecting on advance and retreat (my actions)" means not having lost the Way.<br>
<br>
六四：观国之光，利用宾于王。<br>
劝-辅助：劝说国家宽宏，利于做君王的宾客。<br>
Facilitate: Persuade the state to be broad-minded; favorable to serve as a guest to the king.<br>
《象》曰："观国之光"，尚"宾"也。<br>
《象》传说："劝说国家宽宏"，举荐"宾客"。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "Persuade the state to be broad-minded" means recommending the "guest."<br>
<br>
九五：观我生，君子无咎。<br>
示范：借鉴我，赞许，君子没有错咎。<br>
Demonstration: Take me as a reference, giving approval; for the noble one there is no blame.<br>
《象》曰："观我生"，观民也。<br>
《象》传说："借鉴我，赞许"，是说给人民示范。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "Taking me as a reference, giving approval" means demonstrating to the people.<br>
<br>
上九：观其，生，君子无咎。<br>
劝-勉励：辅助教导，省察，君子没有错咎。<br>
Encourage: Facilitating instruction by examining; for the noble one there is no blame.<br>
《象》曰："观其生"，志未平也。<br>
《象》传说："辅助教导，省察"，是说树立尚未周遍。<br>
The Xiang commentary says: "Facilitating instruction by examining" means the establishment has not yet reached everywhere.<br>
