《师》<br>
徒众 (Mass) - 老师 (Mentor)<br>
<br>
贞丈人吉，无咎。<br>
翻译：与贤明长者对正，吉利；（综合衡量并相应处置）没有过错。<br>
英文：Align with wise elders, auspicious; (weighing matters for synthesis and handling them appropriately), no blame.<br>
参考义项：（太师德化）䢦，率领，引导 / （太傅综合礼法）律，衡量，处置<br>
<br>
《象》曰：地中有水，《师》。君子以容民畜众。<br>
庸常：大地中藏有水源，这就是师卦。君子通过教导百姓，容蓄兵众。<br>
Common: Water is stored within the earth; this is Shi. Therefore the noble one, by teaching the people, accommodates the armed multitude.<br>
<br>
初六：师出以律，否臧凶。<br>
律-约束：军队靠纪律舍弃（赃物），贪吝赃物有凶险。<br>
Constraint / Informed: An army must forgo (plunder) through discipline; to be greedy for plunder is perilous.<br>
德语图式：Disziplin<br>
英文直译：discipline / restraint under rules<br>
<br>
九二：在师中吉，无咎，王三锡命。<br>
率-遵循：施行师祭符合吉礼，没有过错，君王三次赐予诏命。<br>
Conform / Conform: The performance of the *shiji* (pre-campaign sacrificial rite) adhered to the prescribed *jili* (auspicious rites) without error, prompting the sovereign to issue three separate imperial decrees of commendation.<br>
德语图式：Regelbefolgung<br>
英文直译：rule-following / adherence to the prescribed rites<br>
<br>
<br>
六三：师或舆尸，凶。<br>
遂-专擅：统帅迷乱，下属夷由，凶险。<br>
Usurp / Deformation: The commander is muddled, subordinates are hesitant; ominous.<br>
德语图式：Anmaßung<br>
英文直译：arrogation / usurpation of command<br>
<br>
六四：师左次，无咎。<br>
率-劝导：将领使犹疑趑趄的振作，没有过错。<br>
Persuade / Reformer: persuasion: Encouraging those who are hesitant or wavering to rouse themselves; there is no blame.<br>
德语图式：Ermutigung<br>
英文直译：encourage<br>
<br>
六五：田有禽，利执言，无咎。长子帅师，弟子舆尸，贞凶。<br>
率领：田野中有擒获（俘虏），利于抓住而进行讯问，没有过错。长子统率军队，弟弟运载神主牌，保持这种状态会有凶险。<br>
Lead / Uniform: There are captives in the fields; it is favorable to seize and interrogate them, no blame. The eldest son leads the army with true command, the younger brother carries the spirit tablet; if this persists, there will be danger.<br>
德语图式：Lenkung<br>
英文直译：steering / directing / guidance<br>
<br>
上六：大君有命，开国承家，小人勿用。<br>
表率：扶助君主佑助百姓，开拓邦国辅佐采邑；狭隘的人疏忽于庸常。<br>
Exemplar / Transform: Assisting the sovereign and aiding the people, expanding the state and supporting one’s fief—such routine yet vital duties are often overlooked by those of narrow vision.<br>
德语图式：Vorbild<br>
英文直译：model / exemplar / role model<br>
