《师》<br>
徒众 (Mass) - 老师 (Mentor)<br>
<br>
贞丈人吉，无咎。<br>
翻译：与贤明长者对正，吉利；（衡量并依律处置）没有过错。<br>
英文：Align with wise elders, auspicious; (weighing matters and handling them to synthesize), no blame.<br>
参考义项：（太师德化）䢦，率领，引导 / （太傅综合礼法）律，衡量，处置<br>
<br>
《象》曰：地中有水，《师》。君子以容民畜众。<br>
裕-教导：大地中藏有水源，这就是师卦。君子通过更新百姓，培养兵众。<br>
Teach: Water is stored within the earth; this is Shi. Therefore the noble one, by renovating the people, cultivates the armed multitude.<br>
<br>
初六：师出以律，否臧凶。<br>
律-约束：军队靠纪律舍弃（赃物），贪吝赃物有凶险。<br>
Constraint / Informed: An army must forgo (plunder) through discipline; to be greedy for plunder is perilous.<br>
德语图式：Disziplin<br>
英文直译：discipline / restraint under rules<br>
<br>
九二：在师中吉，无咎，王三锡命。<br>
率领：祭祀众多（神祇）符合吉礼，没有过错，君王三次赐予诏命。<br>
Leading / Reformer: The performance of rites for the various (deities) conforms to the auspicious ritual protocols, no blame; the king has issued imperial decrees bestowing honors upon three times.<br>
德语图式：Führung<br>
英文直译：leadership / command / conduct<br>
<br>
<br>
六三：师或舆尸，凶。<br>
率-遵循：统帅惑乱，下属夷由，凶险。<br>
Comply / Deformation: The commander is confused, subordinates are hesitant; ominous.<br>
德语图式：Kadavergehorsam<br>
英文直译：blind obedience (lit. cadaver obedience)<br>
<br>
六四：师左次，无咎。<br>
尊崇：将领佐助趋奉，没有过错。<br>
Honor / Conform: The commander flanked the left, attending with the utmost deference (of a disciple), there is no blame.<br>
德语图式：Ehrfurcht<br>
英文直译：reverence / awe / deference<br>
<br>
六五：田有禽，利执言，无咎。长子帅师，弟子舆尸，贞凶。<br>
引导：田野中有擒获（俘虏），利于抓住而进行讯问，没有过错。长子统率军队，弟弟运载神主牌，保持这种状态会有凶险。<br>
Lead / Uniform: There are captives in the fields; it is favorable to seize and interrogate them, no blame. The eldest son leads the army with true command, the younger brother carries the spirit tablet; if this persists, there will be danger.<br>
德语图式：Lenkung<br>
英文直译：steering / directing / guidance<br>
<br>
上六：大君有命，开国承家，小人勿用。<br>
表率：大君富有声名，开创拯救国与家，小人不要这样。<br>
Exemplar / Transform: The great ruler is highly prestigious: to found and save states and houses; petty people should not act like this.<br>
德语图式：Vorbild<br>
英文直译：model / exemplar / role model<br>
