吉。原筮元。永贞无咎。不宁方来，后夫凶。<br>
翻译：吉利。推究占卜，玩味。长久守正无过错。贪吝竟违拗治理，谋求对抗，有凶险。<br>
英文：Auspicious. Examine the divination (to analyze), to savor, long-lasting uprightness has no blame. Avarice lead to defying disciplinary measures and attempts at confrontation, dangerous.<br>
参考义项：（太卜分析卜筮）箄，比拟（也作“笓”），分析 ；（太保召集）批，触犯，抛弃<br>

《象》曰：地上有水，《比》。先王以建万国，亲诸侯。<br>
亲近：地面上有水，这就是比卦。先王以此建立万国，辛苦诸侯。<br>
Close: Water is on the earth; this is Bi. The earlier kings established ten thousand states and make the feudal lords put in a lot of hard work.<br>
